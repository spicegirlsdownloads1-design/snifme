# Trufflehog Intelligence v7.3 — Offline Service-Role & Workflow Comparison

**24 October 2026 · Chrome Manifest V3 · no new permissions · fully local offline HAR analysis**

## What changed

The existing **Overview → Independent HAR coverage → 5. Compare two reviewed HARs (offline)** now shows a **service-role matrix** in addition to the v7.2 stage comparison:

- Fixed-category DAM vendor/API gateway/object storage/CDN/unknown service roles, counted across every eligible classified HAR entry, not just the first 240 displayed timeline events.
- Role × event-stage observations: for example an export-request clue on a vendor service or media response on a CDN hostname category.
- A bounded, strictly **non-causal** nearby-role sequence count. Pairs are consecutive classified HAR entries no more than five positions apart. This does not establish request linkage, asset identity or any authenticated download.
- Normalised per-1,000-HTTP-entry role rates, with the earlier five user-declared comparability questions and coverage warnings.
- Dedicated redacted service-role CSV export. The existing comparison JSON includes these new fields. No source names or IDs are included.

The report uses only **fixed labels, integers, and fixed warnings**. Source URLs, hosts, paths, filenames, signing values, credentials, POST content, timestamps, cookies, trace IDs and asset identifiers never appear in the added outputs. Hostname classification is temporary, in-memory, and does not imply a shared DAM backend. Custom domains are usually **Other/unknown service**.

## Windows workflow

1. Back up your Evidence Vault data. Extract into the *existing* unpacked extension folder, then select **Reload** at `chrome://extensions`.
2. Open **Overview → Independent HAR coverage**. Select two locally exported, permissioned browser HARs (each max 12 MB and 12,000 entries).
3. Complete **1. Inspect HAR privacy locally** for the first and **4. Inspect second HAR privacy locally** for the second. Treat both raw HARs as sensitive even if no warnings appear.
4. Declare comparison context (same site, actions, account/role, asset scope and DevTools coverage). Unknown defaults are retained.
5. Click **5. Compare two reviewed HARs (offline)**. Scroll to **Observed service-role distribution**, **Service role × observed stage** and **Nearby cross-role observations**. Export redacted CSV or JSON if useful.

## Interpretation limits

- Role detection requires known service domains. A custom brand domain used for S3/CloudFront/Bynder is not reliably attributable from host shape alone.
- A high count of `DAM/API to CDN` adjacent entries does not mean those entries shared a resource or were generated by one user's action.
- Sampling context and HAR capture completeness can change results without any underlying server change.
- Extension-added reports have strict fixed-label export guarantees; other pre-existing export screens retain their own independent privacy contracts.
